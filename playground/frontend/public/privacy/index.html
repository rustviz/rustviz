Why we record

RustViz is a research project at the University of Michigan. We record how people use the tutorial and the playground to learn how people come to understand Rust's ownership and borrowing, and to find out what RustViz explains badly or cannot draw.

What is recorded

  • Which pages you open, how long they are in front of you (and in use), and how far you scroll.
  • Which RustViz diagrams you look at, which parts of a diagram you point at (for example the arrow showing that s is moved) and for how long, and the path of your pointer over a diagram, sampled ten times a second.
  • In the playground: which examples you pick, how much you edit, and each run's result, for example the rustc error codes (such as E0382) or that RustViz could not draw the program. The text of the programs you run in the playground, and of what you are editing (at most once every ten seconds), is recorded too: each distinct program once.
  • Your screen size (rounded to 100 pixels), your browser's language setting and your time zone offset, and the website you came from (its host name only).
  • Three random IDs: one for this page view, one for this visit (it changes after 30 minutes without activity), and one kept in your browser's local storage so that visits from the same browser can be linked. They are random numbers, not derived from anything about you.

What is not recorded

  • No name, e-mail address or account. We never ask who you are.
  • No IP address: our event collector does not record it, and its request logs are excluded from storage. [Maintainer: confirm once deployed.   • No cookies and no third-party analytics or advertising scripts.
  • Nothing you type outside the playground's editor. The tutorial's own example programs are not sent: a diagram is identified by a fingerprint of its program.

Turning it off

  • Use Your data (in the playground's info pane, or the button in the corner of a tutorial page) and choose Turn off. Recording stops, and the random ID is deleted from your browser. Turn off and delete my data also asks us to delete what was recorded with that ID.
  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is recorded at all.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser also removes the ID.
